## Phase 1: Priority B outreach (week 1, about 23 people)

Start here because these people already hold teachable knowledge.

1. **Shortlist 8 to 10 from Priority B first.** Order: teacher-creators (Tanya Price, Samantha Kopp Tabor, Sharice Richards, Kaaren Thompson, Leslie Wilks), authors (Serafin Heredia, Deanna Zlobinski), then the rest.
2. **Send connection requests in batches of 5 per day.** Use the template from the outreach sheet, personalised with one specific detail from each profile. Avoid the sensitive details noted in the analysis.
3. **Wait 2 to 3 days for acceptance.** Then send the "after they accept" message asking for 20 minutes. Keep it to what they have tried and where it gets hard. Don't pitch in the first message.
4. **Send one follow-up after a week, then stop.** The sheet says to move on if there is no reply after the follow-up.
5. **Log every contact** (status, date, response) in `Screening status` in the workbook. Log sends in `campaigns/campaigns.csv` (names only, per the existing convention).

**Target by end of week 1:** 5 to 8 requests sent, 3 or more accepted.

## Phase 2: Calls and feedback (weeks 2 to 3)

1. **Run each 20-minute call with the same three questions:** what did you try recently, where did the process stall, what would you create next.
2. **Demo only if they ask.** If they do, show a short sample book on a topic from their own field.
3. **Capture feedback in a shared table:** prospect, segment, pain point, would they use it (yes / maybe / no), what would stop them.
4. **Send a thank-you within 24 hours**, with one next step.

**Target by end of week 3:** 4 to 6 calls done, feedback logged for each.

## Phase 3: Priority A and C (weeks 3 to 4)

1. **Priority A (about 15 aspiring creators):** send a shorter version of the message. Use them for early feedback and a few sign-ups. Don't spend long on them if they don't reply.
2. **Priority C (about 12 experienced educators, coaches and operators):** ask for referrals and 30-minute expert interviews, not product use. Look at the connectors (Ebere Christian, Anirban Das, Geo Derice) as possible partners, and treat competitors with care.

**Target by end of week 4:** 2 to 3 early users active, and a short list of referrals.

## Decision points

- **After Phase 2:** If at least 3 of the calls say they'd use it, decide on the pilot offer (see blocker 2) and draft a pilot email.
- **If fewer than 2 say yes:** Look at the feedback before contacting more people. Adjust the message, the segment, or the product pitch first.
- **Stop rule:** no more than 2 follow-ups per person, and no contact with anyone who asks to be removed.

## Risks and how to handle them

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Promising a free or paid pilot before pricing is settled | Hold all pilot wording until blocker 2 is closed. |
| Sounding like a sales pitch to creators | Lead with their work and questions, not the product. |
| Messaging the wrong person | Blocker 4 (verify URLs and names) before sending. |
| Sensitive personal stories in the snippets | Refer only to professional or creative work. |
| Overlap with the Thilan, Chaudri and Annachi threads | Keep this list separate, and check the shared queue before each batch so the same person isn't contacted twice. |
