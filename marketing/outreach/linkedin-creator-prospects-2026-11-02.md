## 2. Observations

- **Priority A is weakest on evidence of publishing.** Most are self-described "aspiring" creators with small networks (e.g., 10 to 255 followers). They fit the "build an audience" angle but may not have a body of knowledge to digitise yet. Use them for early feedback, not paid pilots.
- **Priority B has the best fit for Mentible's core use case**: people who already hold teachable knowledge (teacher creators such as Tanya Price, Samantha Kopp Tabor, Sharice Richards, Kaaren Thompson; authors such as Serafin Heredia and Deanna Zlobinski; Lisa Tannert and Mau Patel). Start with these.
- **Priority C has more value as referral or interview sources than as users.** Todd Beuckens, Sean Berg, Bruce Bradley, Autumn Konopka and Janet Lee are experienced and could give critical feedback. Ebere Christian, Anirban Das and Geo Derice may be competitors or channel partners. Treat them as connectors.
- **Data-quality flags to check before outreach:**
  - Some profile URLs end in patterns that look like placeholders (`emily-h12345`, `orobosaotasowie1234`). Confirm each URL resolves to the right person.
  - Partial or generic names ("Emily H.", "Mia K.", "Khamidah Edu Creator") need the full name confirmed.
  - Several locations are "not shown", so time-zone and market fit are unknown for those.
- **Sensitive content:** a few snippets touch on grief, mental-health advocacy, and a veteran's story (Deanna Zlobinski, Khadijah Abdullahi, Serafin Heredia). Keep outreach to their professional or creative work and avoid referencing those personal details.

## 3. Outreach guidance from the workbook

- **Sender:** the founder, from a real personal LinkedIn account, with the Mentible role stated clearly. A company page supports credibility but does not replace personal outreach.
- **Sequence:** connection request, then a 20-minute conversation request after acceptance (it may later ask about a paid pilot), then one follow-up. If there is no reply after the follow-up, move on.
- **Conversation focus:** what they have tried, where the process stalls, and what they want to create next. Do not pitch in the first exchange.
- **Qualification questions** are in the Prospects sheet, column H, one per person.
