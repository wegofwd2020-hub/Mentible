💡 What does it do?
→ You describe your book idea
→ AI writes chapters for you
→ You review, edit, collaborate
→ Done in HOURS

🎯 Perfect for:
✅ Entrepreneurs writing their first book

→ Sold to 150+ countries
→ You earn royalties

🎯 Perfect for:
✅ Authors who already write
✅ Books optimized for discovery on Amazon
✅ Anyone wanting recurring royalty income
✅ Print book publishers
✅ Audiobook creators

💰 Cost:

• Amazon keeps 30-65% of sales (you get 35-70%)
