# Moving a Studio book into Projects

Hi Sridhar — you can now turn a book you wrote in **Studio** into a **Project**
(the validated, source-backed side of Mentible) in a couple of taps. Here's how.

---

## Before you start

- **Sign in** to your Mentible account (the same one your Studio books are under).
- **Use the latest version:**
  - **Web:** open [mentible.app](https://mentible.app) and do a hard refresh (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R).
  - **Android:** install the latest app build (v0.2.51 / vc63 or newer).

> Your Studio books live on the device/browser where you created them, so run this
> on that same device.

---

## How to convert a book

You'll find the button in either place:

- **On a computer / wide screen:** go to the **Books** tab, click a book to select it,
  and in the panel on the right click **"To Project."**
- **On a phone, or the book's own page:** open the book (**Convert to Project** button
  near the bottom, under Share).

Then:

1. A dialog explains what will happen — read it, and tap **Convert**.
2. Mentible creates a **new Project** with your book's **outline** and turns each
   generated topic into a **draft**.
3. When it's done, tap **Open Project** to jump straight to it.

That's it — the Project appears under your **Projects**, ready to work on.

---

## What carries over — and what doesn't

**Comes across:**
- The book's **title** and full **outline** (subjects → topics).
- Each topic's written content — **headings, prose, diagrams, and math**.

**Does *not* come across:**
- **Attached images** and **quizzes** (Projects don't have those yet).
- Topics you only outlined but never generated (they're skipped — nothing to import).

---

## Important: the drafts start "ungrounded"

Studio books are written freely — they don't carry **sources**. Projects are all about
**source-backed, validated** knowledge. So right after converting, your imported drafts
are **not yet grounded or validated** — they're a starting point.

To finish turning them into real Project content:

1. Open the new Project and go to **Capture** → **add your sources** (the documents,
   notes, or references the content is based on).
2. Go through each topic under **Drafts / Per Topic**:
   - optionally **regenerate** it so it draws from your sources with citations, **or**
   - if you're happy with the imported text, just **Approve** it (one tap — as the
     owner, you're the validating expert).

---

## Good to know

- **Nothing is deleted.** Your original Studio book stays exactly where it is — this
  makes a **copy** into Projects. You can even run it again.
- You can convert **as many books as you like**, one at a time.
