# Moving your Studio books into Projects

Hi Sridhar — the books you wrote in **Studio** can now be turned into
**Projects** (the validated, source-backed side of Mentible). Here's exactly how.

Quick heads-up: your Studio books live **on the device/browser where you
created them**, so please do this on that same machine (and signed into the same
account).

---

## 1. Get on the latest version

- **Web:** open **mentible.app**, sign in, and hard-refresh — **Ctrl + Shift + R**
  (Windows) or **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac).
- **Android:** install the latest build (**v0.2.62 / vc74 or newer**) from the
  landing page download.

## 2. Convert a book

1. Go to the **Library** tab — your books appear there.
2. **Tap a book** — a details panel opens on the right.
3. In that panel, tap the **🔧 Book tools** icon (next to **Read**). This opens
   the book's home screen.
4. Scroll down and tap **Convert to Project** (near the bottom, under **Share**).
5. A dialog explains what happens — read it and tap **Convert**.
6. When it's done, tap **Open Project** to jump straight in.

Repeat for each book. Nothing is deleted — this makes a **copy** into Projects;
your Studio book stays exactly where it is.

> **If you don't see the 🔧 Book tools icon:** you're probably on an older
> cached version. Hard-refresh again (step 1). As a backup, you can go straight
> to **mentible.app/books** (signed in) — tap a book there → **Convert to
> Project**.

## 3. Finish the Project (important)

Studio books were written freely, so they don't carry **sources** — and Projects
are all about **source-backed, validated** content. Right after converting, your
imported drafts are a **starting point**, not yet grounded or validated. To
finish each one:

1. Open the new Project → **Capture** → **add your sources** (the documents,
   notes, or references the content is based on).
2. Go through each topic under **Drafts / Per Topic** and either:
   - **Regenerate** it so it draws from your sources with citations, **or**
   - if you're happy with the imported text, just **Approve** it (one tap —
     as the owner, you're the validating expert).

---

## What carries over — and what doesn't

**Comes across:** the book's **title** + full **outline** (subjects → topics),
and each topic's **headings, prose, diagrams, and math**.

**Does *not*:** attached **images** and **quizzes** (Projects don't have those
yet), and topics you only outlined but never generated (nothing to import, so
they're skipped).
