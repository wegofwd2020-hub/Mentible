Thanks for joining Mentible. It's a studio for turning your expertise into expert-validated, traceable knowledge — books, guides, and short-form content drafted by AI from your sources, with every claim cited back to one.

Four phases, one traceable thread:

Capture — paste transcripts, notes, and links; we organize them into labelled sources.
Create — AI drafts an outline and content from those sources only, inventing nothing.
Validate — you review each version, leave feedback, and approve or withdraw it.
Share — publish the approved master to your chosen formats, plus social derivatives.

💬 Tell us what to fix next

You can send feedback from inside the app in a few seconds — no email needed. Here's how:

1. Make sure you're signed in, then tap the round speech-bubble button (💬 "Send feedback") near the top of the app.
2. Pick a type — Bug, Feature suggestion, Content quality, and so on.
3. Write a few words on what happened or what you'd like. The more specific, the better.
4. Choose whether you'd like us to follow up by email, then hit Send. That's it — it reaches us directly.

Don't see the button? It only shows once you're signed in. Please don't include passwords or private customer data.

Prefer email? Reach us anytime at support@kaundinyalabs.com.
